TIMING · 03

Trigger Effects

GOAT Format can recognise that a Trigger Effect has been met even in the middle of a Chain, or after the monster has moved away.

Recognition is not lost

In GOAT Format, a Trigger Effect can be recognised as having met its condition during the resolution of a Chain. Moving the monster to a different location afterwards does not stop that effect from being placed on the next Chain.

EXAMPLE

Magician of Faith is flipped face-up by Desert Sunlight while Compulsory Evacuation Device is resolving. Even though Compulsory later returns it to the hand, its Flip Effect was recognised and activates on the next Chain.

Triggers can be met in the Deck

A Trigger Effect may also be recognised while its monster is in the Deck. This is one of the unusual GOAT Format timing rules and matters when an effect moves a monster to the top of a Deck.

EXAMPLE

If Night Assailant is discarded for A Feather of the Phoenix and then placed on top of the Deck by that effect, its Trigger Effect is still recognised and starts a new Chain.

Quick check

  • Check whether the trigger condition was met while the Chain resolved.
  • Do not assume a move to the hand, Deck or another location prevents the trigger.
  • After the current Chain resolves, build a new Chain for recognised Trigger Effects.
AT A GLANCE In GOAT Format, the game can remember the trigger.

Changing the monster’s location later in the Chain does not erase a recognised Trigger Effect.
