ACTIONS · 04

Priority

After a Summon or Chain finishes resolving, the turn player has the first opportunity to act — including with an Ignition Effect.

What Priority means

Current fast-effect timing still applies in GOAT Format, but the turn player has one additional option. When a Summon or Chain has finished resolving, they may use a fast effect or activate a monster’s Ignition Effect before the opponent gets a response window.

This rule changes Ignition Effects, not the normal handling of Trigger Effects.

Common table situations

TRIBE-INFECTING VIRUS

After it is Summoned, its controller can activate its effect before the opponent can use Book of Moon.

CANNON SOLDIER

After Sangan is Summoned while Cannon Soldier is already face-up, the turn player can activate Cannon Soldier and Tribute Sangan before the opponent can use Torrential Tribute.

BREAKER THE MAGICAL WARRIOR

Its Spell Counter effect triggers first. Once that Chain has resolved, its controller can use Breaker’s Ignition Effect before the opponent gains a new response window.

Quick check

  • Ask whether a Summon or Chain has just finished resolving.
  • If it has, the turn player acts first.
  • In GOAT Format, that first action may be an Ignition Effect.
AT A GLANCE The turn player may use an Ignition Effect first.

This is the key difference from the current fast-effect timing you may be used to.
