BATTLE · 02

Damage Step

The Damage Step has six distinct timings in GOAT Format. Knowing the current timing is the fastest way to know what may happen next.

The six timings

01Start of the Damage Step

Some effects activate before a face-down attack target is flipped face-up.

02Before Damage Calculation

The attacked monster is flipped face-up, if needed; certain effects can activate.

03Damage Calculation · Part 1

Relevant effects and ATK/DEF modifiers resolve before battle results are set.

04Damage Calculation · Part 2

Battle Damage is applied and effects triggered by Battle Damage can activate.

05After Damage Calculation

Monsters are destroyed by battle; Flip Effects and many battle-triggered effects activate.

06End of the Damage Step

Monsters destroyed by battle are sent to the Graveyard and destruction triggers can activate.

One Chain per timing

In GOAT Format, each Damage Step timing normally allows only one Chain. After that Chain resolves, the duel moves forward to the next timing unless that resolution itself triggered another effect.

EXAMPLE

During Damage Calculation, Limiter Removal is activated and Solemn Judgment is chained. After that Chain resolves, battle continues; there is no new opportunity to start another ordinary Chain in the same timing.

What can respond?

The Damage Step is restrictive. Counter Traps and monster effects that negate activations can be used at the appropriate timings. Generic Quick-Play Spells, Normal Traps and Continuous Trap effects cannot be used there just because they have Spell Speed 2.

AT A GLANCE Name the timing first.

It determines which effects may be activated and whether battle has already been decided.
